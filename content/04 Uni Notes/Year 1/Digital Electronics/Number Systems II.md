## Example
 e.g. Negate + $6_{10}$ in binary.
##### Step 1: Write down $+6_{10}$ in binary
$0 1 1 0$
##### Step 2: Do bit-wise inversion
$1 0 0 1$
##### Step 3: Add 1
$1 0 1 0$

2's complement is $1 0 1 0$ which is $(-6)_{10}$

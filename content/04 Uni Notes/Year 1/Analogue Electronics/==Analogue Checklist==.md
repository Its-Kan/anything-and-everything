## Analogue Electronics
- [x]  Determine the currents and voltages in any linear AC or DC circuit with up to two sources (voltage or current sources) and three passive components (resistors, capacitors and inductors) *For AC circuit, be able to plot the results on a phasor diagram*
- [x] Determine the power supplied by any given source in a DC circuit (or the power dissipated by any component or source) in a circuit with up to two independent sources and three passive components
- [x] Work out the rms average of any given signal
- [x] Derive the time-constant of circuit charging or discharging a capacitor through a resistor, and work out how long it takes to charge/discharge the capacitor to any given voltage
- [x] Derive and use the formulas for charging an inductor from a voltage source or charging a capacitor from a current source
 For any two-terminal AC or DC circuit with up to two independent sources and three passive components, determine the Thevenin equivalent network. *Note that if there are no voltage or current sources, this is equivalent to finding the equivalent impedance of a network of passive components* 🆔 59KDxT
 Determine the DC currents flowing and DC voltages in a circuit containing up to two diodes, with voltage and current sources and resistors. *Using iterative solving techniques if accuracy is required* 🆔 GdJd7R
- [x] Derive the equations for the gain of basic inverting and non-inverting op-amp amplifier circuits, and use superposition to determine the gain of summing and differential amplifiers
- [x] Determine the output voltage of an amplifier circuit using an op-amp with up to four passive components and two input sources (voltage or current sources) *This includes differential amplifiers, summing amplifiers as well as basic inverting and non-inverting amplifiers*
 Given any passive circuit with one input and output and up to three passive components (resistors, capacitors or inductors) determine the frequency response of the circuit, and the frequencies of the poles and zeros in the response. *Also, be able to plot the result on a Bode plot, together with the Bode approximation to the amplitude response of any such circuit For second-order systems, be able to express the pole frequencies either as real or complex frequencies, or in terms of the resonant frequency and Q-factor* 🆔 ikeJ2I
- [x] For a circuit with one pole and no zeros, determine the loss at any frequency given the 3-dB bandwidth or the frequency of the pole.
- [x] Determine the 3-dB bandwidth of a second-order bandpass filter from the component values on a circuit diagram

- [x] Describe the effects of the finite open-loop gain of a real op-amp on the characteristics of the op-amp

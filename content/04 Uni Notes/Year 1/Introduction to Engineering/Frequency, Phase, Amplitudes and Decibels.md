# Waves

**Period, T, in s** - smallest time after which a waveform exactly repeats
**Frequency, f, in Hz or $\omega$, in rad/s** - inverse of the period. Multiply by $2\pi$ to convert to rad/s.
**Phase, $\theta$, in $\degree$** - "position" of wave

# Revolutions

**Angular frequency, $\omega$, in rad/s** - rate of change of angles over time

![[04 Uni Notes/Year 1/Analogue Electronics/xPasted Images/Pasted image 20240517034133.png]]

# Averages

**Mean** - add up elements, divide by number of elements
**Mode** - most common value
**Median** - when elements are ordered, the middle value
**RMS** - take the mean of each element squared, then square root

# Amplitude and Power

- 1J of energy is required to move 1C of charge through 1V of potential difference.
- 1W of power corresponds to 1A flowing through 1V of potential difference

# Mean Power

If voltage and current are not constant, power equations only give us instantaneous power. We can just get the average value of the square of the current.

Using the hydraulics analogy:

- Current is flow
- Voltage is pressure

>$V_{1} - V_{2}  {\alpha}  I$ 
>${\Delta}V = IR$ 
>Ohm's law!

Everything in electrical circuits is concerned with ${\Delta}V$ instead of $V$, so we can ground two points in a complete circuit to make the current the same throughout. This can simplify circuits if we just focus on a certain section. HOWEVER, this isn't the same as the mains earth, which has a set safe voltage.  

>${\Sigma} {\Delta}I = 0$
>${\Sigma} {\Delta}V = 0$
>Kirchhoff's Laws!

OK these laws aren't perfect. Ohm's law works for an ideal resistor, however that doesn't exist. Of course, resistance changed based on temperature. It's not too bad though.

KIRCHHOFF THOUGH? EM induction kinda fucks it all up. Voltage law doesn't work when you have a changing magnetic field. Propagating radio waves will affect every circuit. However, it still works as approximates.

A resistor labelled 333 is NOT a 333$\Omega$ resistor. It's $33*10^{3} {\Omega}$. Obviously. Same with capacitors but instead of kilo, it's in pico, or $33*10^{-12}$.

The bands on a resistor are the same, but with c o l o u r s. There are 4/5 bands, first ones are the system just stated, and the last one is a tolerance band. It's the percentage of what the resistance is in.

- Black: 0
- Brown: 1 / 1%
- Red: 2 / 2%
- Orange: 3
- Yellow: 4
- Green: 5 / 0.5%
- Blue: 6 / 0.25%
- Violet: 7 / 0.1%
- Gray: 8
- White: 9

So a red, grey, violet, orange, thick brown resistor would be $287*{10^3}$, with 1% tolerance. 

In the written system, R would be a decimal point. For the colours, we need new ones for $10^{-1}$ and $10^{-2}$.

- Gold: -1 / 5%
- Silver: -2 / 10%

Anyways! You have some responsibilities now, crazy. Thankfully you only have to do this for one society. In short, you are the main communicator/do-er of the society. You carry most of the decisions the president has, as well as communicating with people outside the society if and when needed. If you need specific details on more admin stuff, the [YorkSU Resource Hub](https://resource-hub.yorksu.org/home) has a lot of things written up that you can read, However YorkSU will email you about the important stuff.

So here's the list of things I do (badly, but still do), and how I do them:
## Weekly Email
Probably the most obvious thing you see every week, however it's the most prone to data leaks. That's your job too! Make sure you don't accidentally leak everyone's emails. All the emails are stored on a Mailing List spreadsheet in the Google Drive. This year it's is stored in the *2024/25* folder. As soon as the semester ends and you have access to the drive, **DELETE THE RESPONSES TO 2024/25 MAILING LIST**. Don't delete the form, but clear it and the spreadsheet of any and all emails. Failure to do so is a data leak! Oh, and make sure to put the society email on the mailing list (I forgot to oops).

Some people will register interest in the society through YorkSU's website. Under the *Student Dashboard*, click *Manage* next to Cosplay Society, and then click on *Members* and *Interested Members*. If you want, copy paste those emails to the spreadsheet. Remember people might want to unregister their interest, so make sure to check! 

So, all emails are written in plain ol' Gmail. YorkSU prefers if you use their system on their website, but I hate it, so I don't bother:
1. Compose a new email
2. Copy all the emails from the spreadsheet
3. Paste it into the **BCC** section, not To or CC. Failure to do this is a data leak, yay!
4. Go to the bottom right and click the three vertical dots, and select the weekly email template.
5. Replace the name at the bottom with yours
6. Replace the social date and room to the current one (it might change next year, so be careful).
7. Write the email! 

Some tips:
- (Try) and be consistent. 
- Syncing up the Discord announcement and the email is a good idea. If Tao forgets, remind him of the announcements. We used to do every Friday/Saturday.
- You don't need to go as hard as I did into it, but try and be funny! A lot of people aren't on the Discord, so the weekly email is their way of knowing what's going on in the society, it helps to be appear friendly and casual.
## Checking and Sending Emails
The Gmail will be your best-est friend! Make sure you have notifications for emails on for the Cosplay Soc account, almost every email that inbox is important/worth reading. (Oh, and please at least open every email. We have 0 unread messages and I would like to keep it that way kek). 

YorkSU is probably the most frequent thing you'll get emails from, and send emails to. societies@yorksu.org is the address if you need questions about anything really, or if you have any queries you need clarifying (e.g. room booking issues, storage allocations, holding an EGM, etc.).

We get a lot of emails from third parties such as bars or merch companies wanting to sponsor us. Most of the time they don't align with our societies vibe (think bars, clubs, etc). However if there's an external company that might suit the society, then bring them up to committee chat on Discord. If Tao and the rest of committee thinks its a good fit, then you'll need to fill out a sponsorship form. This is in a YorkSU email under *Sponsorships and Student Resource Hub*.

Occasionally, a potential member will email wanting more information about the society. Lucky for you, I wrote a handy template in the spam-and-notes channel. You're welcome. 
## Writing Meeting Notes
Pretty simple, and very informal. Every semester all of committee gets together to discuss stuff like Fresher's Fair, social plans, etc. It's your job to keep track of what's been discussed, what needs to happen, and what you have done. If anything, it's useful for future committees to look back on! Therefore, you can look back on my and previous secretary's meeting notes if you ever need inspiration. 
## Organising the Google Drive
So when the more official handover happens, you'll be able to change the Google Account's password. And therefore, you'll have access to the Google Drive, where you'll store the presentations, photos, re-ratification documents, and meeting notes. 
## Re-ratification
For the society to continue next year, we need to renew our legally binding agreements every year. And guess what, that's your job! The instructions on how to do this are under a YorkSU email under *Re-Ratification 2025/26 is Now Open!*, but it's also starred. In short, you'll need to:
- Rewrite/update our constitution.
- Write a risk assessment (you can copy mine from last year)
- Write a social impact report
- Track our inventory (treasurer should help you with this)
The specifics of what you need to write might change though, so be sure to read through their guide.
## Booking Rooms
So! Every semester, YorkSU will send out an email with a Google Form asking what rooms at what day and time we want booked for the next semester. **V/N/123** is booked until the end of this semester, so you don't need to worry about that, however in around August/September they'll send an email out again. Do this early to ensure we get our room! If needed, you can look at all the available rooms [here](https://www.york.ac.uk/campusservices/avcentre/room/). This was our order of priority last time:
- **G/N/020**
- **V/N/123**

Occasionally you'll need to book out specific rooms for one day for a photoshoot for example. This will need to be done through the university's room booking service [Planon](https://www.york.ac.uk/fmhelpdesk/room_bookings/student/index.cfm). Once the semesterly room booking is processed, you should be able to see the weekly booking in *Book a room or view my bookings*. And then if you need one-off room bookings, you can do so in the same link, and clicking *Make a new booking*. Just follow their instructions, and you should get a response within a day. 

Any issues with this, email roombookings@york.ac.uk.
## Storage
Not explicitly your job, but is good to know. Our storage is in Biology (I think you've been in there already). Make sure you have your student ID on you, then go to Vanbrugh Reception and ask for *The keys for YorkSU Biology Storage*. You'll just need to give them your ID and phone number, and you should be set.

## Name
OddTime
## Subtitle
A visualiser for rhythmic complexity
## Description
OddTime is an audio-based iOS app that visualises rhythmic complexity for ambitious musicians and producers. It translates polyrhythms, irregular time signatures, and tuplet swing into intuitive, grid-based visualisations. You can broaden your rhythmic horizons and move beyond the basics of rhythm without needing to read sheet music. For performers and producers alike! 

**VISUALISE YOUR RHYTHMS**
Current music production tools and DAWs often lock you into regular time, often needing manual, tedious workarounds to get these rhythms down. OddTime uses a dynamic, grid-based interface that simplifies even the most complex rhythmic ideas into an easy-to-understand visualisation.
- Polyrhythm Grid: Visualise a polyrhythm using a grid. Pink is pulse one, purple is pulse two!
- Dynamic Controls: Adjust pulse values quickly with a simple drop-down menu. Switch them around with a single button.
- Precise Playback: Control the tempo with a responsive slider.

**UI/UX FORWARD**
OddTime was created with UI/UX as a priority in development, and is designed to be a professional tool that accentuates clarity and ease of use.
- Grid-Based Interface: Navigate a familiar DAW-style grid sequencer, that makes complex rhythms instantly readable.
- Onboarding: A short, 5-step tutorial on how to use the app is shown the first time the app is launched, allowing a seemless transition into using the app practically.
- Minimalist Design: A modern, purely digital interface in both light and dark modes allows you to focus entirely on usability. 

**BUILT FOR YOU**
OddTime is dedicated to be an ever-developing app, collaborating with the community to build a tool musicians actually want to use. We've listened to ideas and feedback, and are planning to add the following features in future updates:
- Pattern Saving: You will be able to save your custom rhythms, to build your personal collection of patterns
- Automation: The ability to change the visualiser over time, enabling rhythmic transitions.
- Song Library: A library of pre-set rhythms, as well as popular songs synced with the visualiser will be added.

## 2.2.2. Role Description 
The Project Manager is responsible for facilitating the agile development process and guiding the team's efforts toward the successful completion of the software application. He/she is accountable for ensuring that the project's agile lifecycle (see section 4) is executed efficiently from the initial sprint to the final product delivery. Accomplishing this involves several tasks that include:

- Organising and facilitating all key agile ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, sprint reviews, and sprint retrospectives.
    
- Clearly defining and communicating the agenda and objectives for each agile meeting.
    
- Managing the product backlog, which involves translating stakeholder requirements into user stories and prioritizing them for development.
    
- Maintaining a productive and cooperative team environment where all members can contribute effectively and impediments are addressed promptly.
    
- In collaboration with the development and QA teams, providing the plans for each sprint and monitoring burndown charts and other metrics to track progress.
    
- Developing and maintaining the overall project timeline and release plan, ensuring it is met, or making and communicating appropriate modifications as required.
    
- Ensuring that all user stories and deliverables meet the established "Definition of Done" and quality standards at the end of each sprint.
    
- Providing solutions by actively identifying, tracking, and removing impediments that may block team progress.
    
- Facilitating the sprint review process where the team demonstrates completed work to stakeholders for approval.
    
- Serving as the primary point of communication between the project team and stakeholders (e.g., professor, TA), providing regular status reports and updates.
    
- Producing and maintaining the overall project plan, product roadmap, and time schedule.

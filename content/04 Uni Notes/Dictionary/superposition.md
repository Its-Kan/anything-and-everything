## Definition
**In Maths:** If $y_{1}$ and $y_2$ are both solutions to a linear non-homogenous (equals 0) differential equation, then $c_{1}y_{1} + c_{2}y_{2}$ is also a solution.

**In Circuits:** The total voltage/current effect multiple power sources have on a circuit is equal to the sum of each of the power sources on their own when ignoring the other sources (where voltage sources are replaced with a wire and current sources are replaced with a break).

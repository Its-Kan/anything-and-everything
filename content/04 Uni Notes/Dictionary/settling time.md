## Definition
The time it takes for the output to settle to 98% of its final value (or $\pm 2\%$ of its final value for second order), around 4 time constants.
$$T_{s} = 4 \tau = \frac{4}{a}$$
- where $a$ is the distance from the origin to the pole location
$$T_{s} = \frac{4}{\zeta \omega_{n} } = \frac{4}{\alpha}$$
- where $\alpha$ is the distance from the origin to the real part of the pole location.

## Definition
To transform resistors, capacitors, and inductors into the \(s\)-domain (Laplace domain), we use their impedance representations. $$s = \sigma + j\omega$$Below is how each component is transformed:
### Resistor - R
- **Impedance**:  $$Z_R = R$$
  where \(R\) is the resistance in ohms.
### Capacitor - C
- **Impedance**:  
$$  Z_C = \frac{1}{sC}$$
  
  where \(C\) is the capacitance in farads
### Inductor - L
- **Impedance**:  
$$  Z_L = sL$$
  where \(L\) is the inductance in Henries.
### Voltage Source
$$V_{s}=\frac{V_{0}}{s}$$
### Current Source
$$I_{s}=\frac{I _{0}}{s}$$

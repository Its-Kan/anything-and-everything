A type of [[Fourier transform]], which transforms a discrete [[signal]] in its [[time domain]] into its [[frequency domain]] version, but frequency is continuous. 
$$x[n] \leftrightarrow X(\omega)$$
It's a more theoretical tool, but gives an exact frequency analysis. It's an implementation of the [[Fourier transform]] for discrete signals.

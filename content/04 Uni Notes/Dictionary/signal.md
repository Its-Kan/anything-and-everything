A quantity that varies in time (and space).
- An analogue signal is a continuously carrying signal in both amplitude and time.
- A digital signal comprises a discrete set of amplitude levels which change at discrete times.

**Message signals** contains information we want to transfer. This could be an audio/video signal, or digital data from a computer.
**Transmitted signals** are the result of a process called [[modulation]], where the message signal is imprinted on a high-frequency carrier wave.

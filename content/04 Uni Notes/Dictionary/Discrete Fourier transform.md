A type of [[Fourier transform]], which transforms a discrete [[signal]] in its [[time domain]] into its [[frequency domain]] version, in discrete bins.  
$$x[n] \leftrightarrow X[k]$$
It has a finite duration, and assumes the signal is periodic. It comes from the [[Discrete time Fourier transform]], by using [[time truncation]], [[periodisation]] and [[frequency sampling]], and is used practically with computers.

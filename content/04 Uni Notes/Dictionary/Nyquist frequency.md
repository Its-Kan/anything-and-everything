The frequency of half of the sampling rate.
$$\begin{align}
f_{s} &= \frac{1}{\Delta t} [\text{Hz}] \\
f_{Nq} &= \frac{f_{s}}{2} = \frac{1}{2 \Delta t} [\text{Hz}]
\end{align}$$
And with the DFT, the Nyquist frequency is $f_{k}$ where $k = \frac{N}{2}$
$$f_{Nq} = f_\frac{N}{2}[\text{Hz}]$$

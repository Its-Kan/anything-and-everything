## Definition
The average power of a signal over a single period is directly equivalent to the sum of the squares of the coefficients associated with each [[complex frequency variable]].
$$
\frac{1}{T} \int_{\langle T \rangle} |x(t)|^{2} \space dt = \sum^{n=\infty}_{n = - \infty} |c_{n}|^2
$$

## Definition
Speed up or slow down rotational motion, trading off speed for torque. 
- Stepdown gearboxes reduce speed, increase torque
- Setup gearboxes increase speed, reduce torque. 
Specified in terms of gear ratios (no. rotations of input: no. rotations of output)
